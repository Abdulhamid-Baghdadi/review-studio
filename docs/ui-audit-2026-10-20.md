# UI audit, 20 October 2026

Scoping Review Workbench checked against `docs/ui-ux-rules.md` after the changes of 18–20 October: one mode
(scoping), one page per step, 9 steps, Search strategy merged into one page, Lock & register as its own step, the
full-text AI choice on the Full texts page, the AI "How it works with Claude" strip, and links on every PRISMA mention.

**How it was checked:**
- **Measured (script `tests/smoke/a11y.mjs`):** contrast, targets, names, field labels, toggle states, headings and
  sideways scrolling.
  - `npm run smoke` already runs these on every page of the example, light and dark, and at 400 px.
  - This audit also ran them where the smoke test does not go: every page at **320 px**, every page with its **AI
    card open**, every page with the **Help drawer open**, and the **unlocked draft** (editors enabled), in light,
    dark and 320 px.
- **Read in the code and tried in the browser pane:** the pages changed since the last audit, for the heuristic
  rules (words, duplicates, one primary action, feedback).

The audit of 17 October (`docs/ui-audit-2026-10-17.md`) found 29 problems; all were fixed then.

## What already works

- **Every page passes the measured floor** in light, dark, at 400 px and now at 320 px. This includes the draft with
  its editors, the open AI cards and the open Help drawer. No console errors.
- **One Mark done per page:** the step split (Lock & register on its own) removed the last page with two.
- **Every PRISMA mention links** to the guideline's own page. The pages exist: prisma-statement.org/scoping,
  /prisma-search and /prisma-2020.
- **AI choices are where the work is:** titles and abstracts, full texts, getting full texts, charting. Each is off by
  default, and each card says who does what.

- **Your real review:** the 5,683-record copy, in Chrome from a disk folder (speed, and the step changes on an
