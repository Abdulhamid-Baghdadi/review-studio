# Full texts of the example review: sources and licences

These eight articles are shipped unchanged with the finished example, under their open licences. Each is the
publisher's version, downloaded from the open-access link the publisher gives. The copyright stays with the
authors (or as stated in each article). Papers of the example search without an open licence are **not** included;
the example marks them "not retrieved". The SMArT Work trial (Edwardson et al., BMJ 2022, CC BY) is read in its
open HTML version on PubMed Central (PMC9382450) and is linked, not copied.
